How ManakAI works

1. A consultation, not a chat wall

ManakAI asks only the questions it needs to understand your product — using your profile, so nothing is asked twice.

2. A checklist that remembers

The result is a persistent, structured checklist: what applies to you, what you have completed, and what to do next.

3. Sources you can check

Every requirement shows its source: authority, document, section and the date it was last verified. When something is unknown, it says so.

Why you can trust what you see

  • Sourced or not shown. No standard, QCO, fee or lab appears without a traceable official source.
  • Unknown means unknown. When applicability cannot be determined from available information, ManakAI says exactly that — it never guesses.
  • Guidance, not approval. ManakAI explains requirements in plain language. It does not grant, promise or replace BIS certification.
